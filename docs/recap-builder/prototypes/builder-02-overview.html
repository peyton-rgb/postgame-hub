Campaign overview

The Brooks Ghost Amp campaign activated 76 college athletes representing 69 universities across 7 sports, leveraging a mix of feed posts, Reels, and TikTok content to drive awareness and excitement around the launch. Collectively, the athlete roster represented a combined social following of 466.7K+ followers.

The campaign strategically aligned with Brooks' massive Ghost Amp influencer launch on August 1, when 70 athletes went live simultaneously to create a strong wave of social momentum and visibility around the product launch.
