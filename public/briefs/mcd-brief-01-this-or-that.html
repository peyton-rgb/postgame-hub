"This or That"
Drink Quiz

A web quiz asks 10 rapid-fire this-or-that questions, computes your top 3 drinks, and reveals your #1. The athlete runs it live and reacts.

** Very important **

Shoot horizontal frame for vertical

i.e. shoot horizontal and edit for vertical. Every take is filmed 16:9. The vertical 9:16 cut is cropped from the centre of that frame in post. Do not shoot vertical.

This forces 4K

A 9:16 crop keeps the full height of the horizontal frame and only about a third of its width. The vertical video's resolution comes entirely from how tall your horizontal frame is.

Shot 4K  3840×2160  →  crop 1215×2160  →  clears 1080×1920 ✓
Shot 1080  1920×1080  →  crop 608×1080  →  fails 1080×1920 ✗

4K 16:9 is the minimum, not a preference. Shooting 1080p horizontal gives a vertical cut we'd have to upscale.

Framing

9:16 CROP
  • The orange column is all that survives. Centre 32% of your frame. Athlete, product, and every reaction live inside it.
  • Outside the column is horizontal-only material — a second person, the table, the room. Never the main action.
  • Don't crowd top or bottom. The crop keeps full frame height, so vertical headroom is locked at capture.
  • Red band = bottom 27% of the finished vertical, where TikTok and Reels put captions.
  • Turn on your camera's 9:16 guide overlay if it has one.
CaptureHorizontal 16:9, 4K minimum (3840×2160+)
Frame rate24 or 30fps — pick one, stay on it
Vertical out1080×1920, centre crop in post
Horizontal out1920×1080 or better
DeliveryOriginal files off the card. No AirDrop, no texting — that compression is why the first demos came in at 720p.
AudioLav mic or a quiet corner. Room tone under every line makes lines unusable.

Objective

Capture honest, high-energy reaction content built around a live quiz result. The reactions carry the video — the quiz is the engine. The athlete's real #1 drink becomes the payoff.

References

What good looks like — the format, the finish, and the photography. Real location, real product in hand, natural light, athlete relaxed rather than posed stiff.

Before you roll

  • Athlete takes the quiz once, on camera, cold. No practice run — a known result kills the reveal.
  • Phone brightness to max. The screen has to read on camera.
  • Phone held chest height, screen angled to camera, inside the crop column.

Shot List

1Hook0:00–0:03

Phone up to camera, quiz visible. One line setting up the game. Energy high from frame one — there's no ramp-up on a :30.

2Setup0:03–0:06

What they're doing and what's at stake. To camera, not to the phone.

3Rapid fire0:06–0:20

Athlete reads the questions aloud and reacts to each.

  • React out loud on every answer. Silence here is dead footage.
  • Let the hesitations land. "Ugh, that's hard" beats a clean tap.
  • Keep rolling through fumbles.
4Top 30:20–0:23

Athlete ranks their top 3, reasoning out loud.

5The reveal0:23–0:27

Hold the phone steady so the result card reads. Two full seconds minimum — this is the frame the edit builds to.

6Callout0:27–end

React to the result, then deliver the required lines.

Do & Don't

Do

  • One continuous take if you can hold it.
  • Let reactions run long — we can trim, we can't invent.
  • Natural light, face lit from the front.

Don't

  • Don't script the answers. The result has to be honest.
  • Don't cover the phone screen.
  • Don't let the phone drift outside the crop column.

Required Lines

Exact wording. Both spoken on camera, and both appear as on-screen text in post. Get a clean take of each.

"Order using the McDonald's App or in-store digital kiosk"

"Download the McDonald's App for deals, rewards, and local offers."

FTC. #ad and #McDonaldsPartner stay on screen for the full cut. Athlete also discloses the partnership verbally near the top.

Brand Rules

  • Post handles the colours: Arches Gold #FFC72C, Red #DA291C, ink #27251F.
  • The arches are never drawn, traced, or generated. Real logo files only.
  • Real product only. No competitor brands in frame — cups, bags, signage, wrappers. No other logos on wardrobe.
  • No music on set. It contaminates audio and isn't cleared.
  • Music for the edit is not sourced. Do not use unlicensed audio.

Red Bull Dragonberry Energizer — no straws. This drink has a sip lid and is not meant to be used with a straw. No straw appears in any content featuring it — not in the cup, not on the table, not anywhere in frame.
